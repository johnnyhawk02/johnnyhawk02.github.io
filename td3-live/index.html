Hardware test

The open question is what the TD-3 does when the slot it's playing gets overwritten. This sends two very different patterns to the live slot, alternating, so you can hear when a change lands.

  1. Immediate, next loop, or only after reselect? Run the flip test with the TD-3 playing the live slot.
  2. Does the sequencer stop or glitch on a write? Try a short interval (0.3 s).
  3. Clock: repeat with internal clock and with USB clock.
  4. Pitch storage: make a pattern with a rest in the middle and check the next note plays on the right step. If not, switch to "indexed".
  5. Double buffer: write to the spare slot, press it, confirm it switches at the end of the bar.
