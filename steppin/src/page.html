what each slider plays

every drum is a phasor~ split two ways: log~ → cycle~ pitch sweep, and 1 − ramp for the decay

samp
Spool loop of a few ms, ring-modulated by a 412 Hz sine with a 300 ms envelope
anny
Random-pitch sine blips: sah~ grabs a 25 Hz ramp 15 times a second, stereo pair
untl
Log sweep clocked at audio rate by the bass steps, so it turns into FM grit
bsStp
Bass sine at step ÷ 4 Hz
tonMt
206 Hz ping on the 5 Hz clock, left channel only
lazr · gabbr
Fast 5 Hz log sweeps from 220 Hz; gabbr is pushed into the clipper by a +0.9 offset
bsClk ×3
Kick-like sweeps on the slow and beat clocks; the third is chopped by a −10 Hz saw
gbStp
Gabber voice whose rate comes from the gabber steps, chopped by the annoying steps
bsDrm
Beat kick with a 5 second curve~ tail
snDrm
Noise through the 8-band filter bank, 80 ms gate, into a 10 ms comb~
snClk
3000 → 200 Hz chirp in 50 ms, 20 ms gate

where the port differs

engine: starts on first click

  • The paste held no saved preset or slider data, so the step, volume and filter values here are starting points I picked.
  • grooveduck isn't a stock Max object. It's emulated as a looping player with a 1 ms fade at each loop point.
  • A DC blocker sits before the output. gabbr and snClk add a +0.9 offset in the patch that would otherwise push the speakers.
  • Sync matches the spacebar exactly, so it also resets the tempo to 800 ms.
